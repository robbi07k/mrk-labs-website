# MRK Labs Wordmark Guidelines

## Proportions and construction
The logo is a single horizontal wordmark: custom geometric `MRK` letterforms followed by custom vector lettering for `Labs`. M, R, and K share a 56-unit cap height with consistent 10-unit strokes; Labs uses open 5-unit strokes and matching capital height. The lockup follows the supplied Concept No. 9 reference: MRK is approximately 55% of its visible width, Labs 41%, and the word gap 4%. Keep the word groups on one baseline. The space before `Labs` is slightly wider than the internal MRK spacing.

The M has a distinct angular center and one small Electric Cyan cut detail. The R has a rounded geometric bowl and a precise diagonal leg, drawn with open strokes rather than a heavy solid shape. The K uses matching angular strokes. `Labs` uses taller, lighter strokes and a single-storey lowercase a. The M shape may be used alone only as the favicon adaptation; do not add a separate symbol to the wordmark.

Preserve the exact spelling and capitalization: `MRK Labs`. Keep the Labs cap height aligned with MRK and the lowercase a/s at a natural x-height. Do not add external or self-hosted fonts.

## Clear space
Keep clear space around the full wordmark equal to at least the height of the M's vertical stem. Do not place other elements inside this area.

## Minimum size
- Full wordmark: 120px wide.
- M letterform adaptation (favicon): 16px high.

## Approved colors
- Primary background: Deep Midnight Navy `#061225`.
- MRK on dark backgrounds: Primary White `#F4F7FC`.
- Labs and the M accent: Electric Cyan `#08B9F2`.
- MRK on light backgrounds: Deep Midnight Navy `#061225`.

The dark-background presentation uses a solid navy field with the wordmark centered. The transparent and white-background variants use dark MRK lettering for contrast. Monochrome variants use one color for every letter. Keep all versions flat, without shadows or gradients.

## Do not
- Add a separate icon or decorative shape to the wordmark.
- Stack the letters or words, or alter the letter proportions, stroke weight, M detail, baseline, or spacing.
- Use gradients, glow, shadows, 3D effects, or additional colors.
- Place the wordmark on a low-contrast background.
- Substitute a flask, brain, rocket, circuit board, hexagon, or unrelated symbol.
